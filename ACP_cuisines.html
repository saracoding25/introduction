Indian Cuisines

The traditional food of India has been widely appreciated for its fabulous use of herbs and spices. Indian cuisine is known for its large assortment of dishes. The cooking style varies from region to region and is largely divided into North Indian and South Indian cuisine. India is famous for its diverse and delicious food.

Bengali Food

Bengali cuisine is famous for its sweet and spicy flavours. Fish, rice, and sweets are popular in this cuisine.

Gujarati Food

Gujarati cuisine is mainly vegetarian and includes dishes like dhokla, thepla, and khandvi.

Mughlai Food

Mughlai cuisine is known for rich curries, biryanis, kebabs, and aromatic spices.

Punjabi Food

Punjabi cuisine is loved for butter chicken, chole bhature, parathas, and lassi.

South Indian Cuisine

South Indian cuisine is famous for idli, dosa, vada, sambar, and uttapam.
